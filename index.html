How to play
  1. Split into 2, 3 or 4 teams. Each team picks a spymaster, who switches to Spymaster view on their phone.
  2. On your turn, your spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number, like "Ocean, 2". Anyone on the team taps the guesses.
  3. Hit your own color and keep guessing (up to the number plus one). Tan is a bystander and ends your turn. Another team's color ends your turn and helps that team.
  4. Black tiles are assassins. With 2 teams, touching one loses the game on the spot. With 3 or 4 teams, that team is knocked out and the rest play on. The last team standing wins.
  5. The starting team has one extra word, and the starting team rotates every game so everyone gets a turn going first. Board size grows with teams: 5×5 for 2, 6×5 for 3, 6×6 for 4. The Big board setting adds a row and a column for playing on a TV or projector.
  6. Vedant's Birthday mode (Classic words plus Vedant words): every team has one hidden Vedant word on its own color, and a few lookalike words are decoys. Spymasters see a gold V on the real ones. Find your team's Vedant word and spin the wheel for a power. Find another team's and whoever tapped it spins for a task.
  7. Powers: Cheat Day means your next wrong guess doesn't end your turn. Saiyan Comeback means a black tile only costs you your next turn instead of the game (lasts until used). Superset means your spymaster can give a two-word clue next turn.
  8. After Dark only: when a Confessions word like "Worst Date" or "Drunk Text" is revealed, the person who tapped it tells their real story about it, or drinks.
